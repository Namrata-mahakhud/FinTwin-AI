## Journey Metrics & KPIs

### Time Estimates

- **Stage 1 (Dashboard):** 30 seconds - 1 minute
- **Stage 2 (Create Scenario):** 2-3 minutes
- **Stage 3 (Run Simulation):** 5-10 seconds (automated)
- **Stage 4 (War Room):** 3-5 minutes (monitoring)
- **Stage 5 (Portfolio Impact):** 2-3 minutes
- **Stage 6 (Risk Heatmap):** 1-2 minutes
- **Stage 7 (AI Recommendations):** 3-5 minutes
- **Stage 8 (Recovery Simulation):** 2-3 minutes
- **Stage 9 (Agent Studio):** 2-4 minutes (optional)
- **Stage 10 (Export Report):** 1-2 minutes

**Total Journey Time:** 15-25 minutes

### Success Criteria

- ✅ 90%+ journey completion rate
- ✅ < 2% abandonment rate
- ✅ 4.5+ user satisfaction score
- ✅ 80%+ recommendation acceptance rate

## Error Handling & Edge Cases

### Scenario 1: Simulation Fails

**Action:** Show error message, allow retry or edit scenario

### Scenario 2: Network Interruption

**Action:** Save progress locally, resume when reconnected

### Scenario 3: Invalid Data

**Action:** Highlight errors, provide suggestions, prevent progression

### Scenario 4: Session Timeout

**Action:** Auto-save, prompt to resume journey
